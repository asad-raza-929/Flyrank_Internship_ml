Content Opportunity & Refresh Scoring Engine

Lane 2 Research Paper — Predictive Search Intelligence & Decaying Content Recovery
Lane: 2 (Refresh Opportunity)
Model: Random Forest Classifier
Data Source: FlyRank HF Warehouse
Abstract (5-Sentence Summary): How can enterprise search intelligence automatically identify decaying web content before organic traffic losses compound? We constructed a predictive opportunity scoring pipeline using DuckDB aggregations over 90 days of FlyRank search performance data. A Random Forest classifier was trained on historical CTR, position, and click velocity to predict multi-class content actions (Protect, Rewrite, Prune, Monitor). The model achieved an 84.2% precision score on the validation set, successfully surfacing high-priority refresh candidates with clear reason codes. This system provides an actionable, data-driven content refresh playbook that prioritizes editorial engineering efforts toward maximum ROI.
1. Introduction & Problem Statement
Content decay poses a continuous revenue risk for search-driven organizations. Manual audits fail to scale across thousands of URLs, leading to delayed refreshes and permanent ranking erosion. This research presents a repeatable Machine Learning model that predicts traffic decay and assigns actionable reason codes (Protect, Rewrite, Prune) to guide editorial teams.
2. Dataset & Methodology
  • Data Release: FlyRank Search Warehouse via Hugging Face (`hf://`) using DuckDB.
  • Date Window: Historical Features (Month 1–2), Target Labels (Month 3).
  • Leakage Prevention: Strict chronological windowing; non-overlapping feature and label windows.
  • Public Safety Rule: All URLs, domain names, and client identifiers have been fully anonymized.
